How to read this page
Pick a cutover date below — usually the day a bid change went live. For every keyword, we take its average daily spend/sales for the days before that date and compare it to the days after. A keyword can look "worse" just because it barely spent anything (a single missing sale swings its ROAS wildly) — that's what the noise cutoff filters out, and why we show two summary numbers: a plain count of keywords, and the same count weighted by how much money was actually behind it. The weighted one is the one to trust.
Blinkit revises recent days as more conversions land — treat any date less than ~2 days old as provisional. Re-check this view in a couple of days before drawing firm conclusions.
