How to read this page
The action date (cutover) is the line everything is measured from: the AI go-live (Chumbak 10 Sep, Voylla 18 Sep) or any day the AI made decisions. Before = the N days up to the day before it; After = from that day for N days (stopping at yesterday). N is the compare window.
The cards show both sides for the same campaigns. Counts (spend, impressions, add to carts, quantity, sales) are compared per day, because the after side may still be filling up and the BAU view can drop event days from either side. ROAS, CTR and CPM are compared as they are.
The % = (after ÷ before − 1). Green = better, red = worse; for CPM lower is better; spend is never coloured, because spending more or less is a choice, not a result.
Other campaigns = every keyword campaign of the brand the AI has never run, over the same days. A festival or a budget cut moves both; the gap between the two lines is what the AI changed.
Days: BAU only leaves out marked event days (Raksha Bandhan, Navratri ...); "All days" puts them back. The Event days panel lists them.
The chart shows the date range you pick (default: 30 days either side of the action date). The grey band is the before window, the coloured band the after window, the line the action date. ▲ = days the AI pushed bid changes (from 18 Sep; the September trial rounds went through the old system and are not marked).
Campaign by campaign = the same before/after for each campaign, with the bids the AI pushed in the after window. Click a column header to sort.
Data = Blinkit keyword ads (Blinkit_Ads_Report), checked against Blinkit's own download on 23 Oct: spend matches to the rupee. Recommendation and banner ads are not in it. Blinkit keeps adding sales to the last ~3 days, so those are provisional. (Blinkit's own Excel repeats the 1st of each month - do not compare against it without removing those rows.)
